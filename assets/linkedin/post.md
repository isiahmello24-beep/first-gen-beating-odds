I transferred to SDSU as a first gen student, so I wanted to know which colleges graduate students like me at rates their student mix does not predict, and whether money explains it.

I modeled first gen six year completion at 1,142 four year colleges, then ranked each school by how far it beat its own prediction.

Spending level and mix explain about 10% of the gap between schools that beat expectations and schools that miss them. The rest is not in the budget data.

CSU Channel Islands was expected to graduate 48% of its 1,105 first gen students. It graduated 71%.

Schools that beat expectations spend 1.4 times as much per student on teaching as schools that fall short. That describes them. It does not show that moving money would change completion.

For a college that wants to do better, the useful output is the list of peers that beat the odds with similar students.
