- 78% of them are top-decile in ≥ 90% of resamples; 98% in ≥ 50%.

- Mean overlap of the top decile between two random resamples: 90.1%.
- Median width of the 95% rank interval: 61 rank positions for top-decile schools, 116 for all schools (out of 1,251).

Reading this: the residual is dominated by the school's own actual-minus-predicted gap, and the coefficients are estimated from 1,251 schools, so resampling the schools moves the coefficients only a little. The bootstrap therefore measures sensitivity of the ranking to *which schools define the prediction line*, not sampling noise in each school's completion rate (Scorecard publishes a point value, not a standard error). Small-cohort schools are still noisier than this interval shows.
