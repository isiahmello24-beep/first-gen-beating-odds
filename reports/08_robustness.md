## (a) Bootstrap of residual ranks: 1,000 resamples of 1,251 schools (seed 20261023)

Each resample refits Model A, re-applies the empirical-Bayes shrinkage and re-ranks every school. `p_top_decile` = share of resamples in which the school lands in the top 10%.

- Point-estimate top decile: 125 schools. Their mean probability of being top-decile across resamples: **93.1%**.
- 82% of them are top-decile in ≥ 90% of resamples; 97% in ≥ 50%.
- 0 schools outside the point-estimate top decile are top-decile in ≥ 50% of resamples (borderline cases).
- Mean overlap of the top decile between two random resamples: 90.5%.
- Median width of the 95% rank interval: 55 rank positions for top-decile schools, 117 for all schools (out of 1,251).

Reading this: the residual is dominated by the school's own actual-minus-predicted gap, and the coefficients are estimated from 1,251 schools, so resampling the schools moves the coefficients only a little. The bootstrap therefore measures sensitivity of the ranking to *which schools define the prediction line*, not sampling noise in each school's own completion rate. That second source is what the Step 6 shrinkage addresses: it pulls small-cohort residuals toward zero in proportion to their binomial variance.
