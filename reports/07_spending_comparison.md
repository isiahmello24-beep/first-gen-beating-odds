Interpretation guide: `welch_p_log` tests whether mean log-dollars differ (robust to the long right tail); `mann_whitney_p` tests whether one group's values tend to be larger. Dollar differences are the finding. Shares describe how the same dollars are split and cannot be read as separate evidence.
