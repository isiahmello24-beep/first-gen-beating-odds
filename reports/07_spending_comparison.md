Interpretation guide: `welch_p_log` tests whether mean log-dollars differ (robust to the long right tail); `mann_whitney_p` tests whether one group's values tend to be larger. A category that differs in dollars but not in share means over-performers spend more on everything; a category that differs in share means they allocate differently.
